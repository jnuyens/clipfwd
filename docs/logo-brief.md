# clipfwd: art guide for the logo

A picture slips from your hands to a faraway machine, quietly, as if it was always there.
One idea, one shape: a mark you can draw from memory in three strokes, never a row of icons.
Show the gesture of handing over: paper in flight, a clip letting go, a corner folding forward.
Calm, crafted curves, generous empty space, one colour with character, a touch of wit.
It may hint at a terminal, but must never look like one; no clip art, boxes or heavy outlines.
It must stay recognisable at fingernail size, in one flat colour, after a two-second glance.
Deliver as vector, with lowercase `clipfwd` beside it, freely usable, in an open-licence font.
