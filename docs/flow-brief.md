# clipfwd: the flow illustration

Result: [`assets/flow.png`](../assets/flow.png).

## What it should show

A screenshot on your laptop ends up, in one smooth move, inside an AI assistant working on a faraway
server. You press paste, and it's there. The viewer should feel how short and natural that path is,
not how complicated it is underneath.

## The four stations, left to right

1. **Your laptop:** a screenshot in your hands. A small picture lifts off the laptop screen, the same
   sheet of paper as in the logo with its folded corner.
2. **The SSH connection:** a single calm line or tunnel between laptop and server. It's private and
   already there; clipfwd only uses it. A secure path, not a padlock.
3. **tmux on the server:** a terminal window that stays open, even when the laptop sleeps. A hint of
   persistence, for example a soft glow or a small moon, still running.
4. **The assistant:** inside that terminal, the picture lands next to a waiting prompt. The names
   "Claude Code", "Codex" and "opencode" as plain text labels, all three equal, in the same font.
   No logos of those products.

## How it should look

- The picture is the hero: the logo's sheet, flying along the path with the same motion feel.
- Same purple and rounded typeface as the logo, one light accent at most, lots of white space.
- One clean line from left to right, without arrows everywhere or network diagram symbols.
- Readable as a wide banner, and simple enough to work stacked vertically on a phone.

## Avoid

- Cloud icons, server racks, cables, padlocks.
- Brand logos of Apple, Anthropic, OpenAI or opencode.
- Technical labels like "Unix socket" or "RemoteForward". At most small captions: "your laptop",
  "ssh", "tmux", "your agent".

## Deliver

Vector, in a wide version and a stacked version, on a light background, and preferably also a dark one.
